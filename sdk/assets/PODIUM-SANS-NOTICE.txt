Podium Sans — optional font extracted locally from Apple firmware

Source: original iPod Photo firmware 1.2.1.
SHA256: 9321189b846a7317f4f575075696056e9a18c79644886a00055a402259c6fadc
Font header offset: 0x467c94; Unicode group header: 0x46783c.
Strike: 16; bitmap height: 17; bit depth: 4.

Apple font data is not included in the repository or public packages.
Media Manager can download the original firmware directly from Apple, verify
its SHA-256, and extract printable ASCII 32–126 locally. Original advances
and bitmap samples are preserved, expanding 4-bit coverage to 8-bit alpha.
A common one-pixel origin accommodates negative left bearings. No glyphs
are resized. Glyph ink rectangles are packed without loss in PODIUM.FNT.
